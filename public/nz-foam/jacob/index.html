What works today

Real council data enters a sales filter built for NZ Foam.

CDI is already querying Auckland Council's high-value building-consent layer live and combining it with verified Canterbury council signals.

Each project can be deduplicated, ranked and tagged against NZ Foam product families such as residential spray foam, commercial closed-cell foam, WarmCore roofing and Rapidfill civil applications.

The score is deliberately transparent: the radar shows the council description, project value, source, likely product candidates, score reasons and recommended next action.

Why it fits NZ Foam
01

Nationwide sales footprint

One national project feed is more useful than separate council searches when the installer and sales network already works across New Zealand.

02

Plans drive quoting

The radar is designed to identify the project early enough for the team to request plans before insulation and roof-envelope decisions are fixed.

03

Wide product fit

Residential, commercial, agriculture, containers, civil, marine, specialty projects and WarmCore give CDI multiple project types to watch.

04

Noise suppression

A consent record is not automatically a lead. Low-value amendments and irrelevant work are intentionally scored down or excluded.

Current status

This is already a working prototype, not a slide deck.

The live product has a working Railway deployment, an Auckland Council API adapter, verified Canterbury records, a national source registry and an NZ Foam-specific scoring layer.

The next build work is expanding council adapters and connecting simple sales feedback fields so the pilot can learn from NZ Foam's decisions.
