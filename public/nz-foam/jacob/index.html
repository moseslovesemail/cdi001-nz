Getting started

How to use Project Radar.

Step 1: Open the radar

Navigate to the Project Radar and browse opportunities by location, project type, fit score and stage.

Use filters to find projects in your area or sector. The fit score (High, Qualify, Monitor) indicates likely NZ Foam product relevance.

Step 2: Read project details

Consent stage: Application, Approved, Building Consent Issued, or Under Construction.

Project value: Published by council; use to estimate install volume.

Source: Always verify with the council link. Source confidence is High (official council source) or Medium (verified report).

Step 3: Verify company and role

Council records show applicant, architect, or engineer names. Verify they are still the decision-maker:

  • Check the builder/contractor website or LinkedIn
  • Call the council to confirm current applicant contact
  • Do not rely on council-published contact details alone

Step 4: Make first contact

Call or email the builder, project manager or architect. Early-stage projects (application or approval) mean specification is not yet locked.

Introduce NZ Foam services. Ask for a site visit or plans to assess insulation needs.

Mark the project as "Contacted" in your review and note the date.

Step 5: Request plans

Plans (building consent drawings, specifications, or scope) let you assess whether spray foam is a fit and what volume/cost is realistic.

Record plans received in your review. If plans are refused, note why and update the outcome.

Step 6: Quote and record

If plans confirm a fit, prepare and submit your quote.

Record in the Project Radar:

  • Quote value: Total NZ Foam project value
  • Outcome: Quoted, Pending, Won, Lost
  • Won revenue: Actual contract value if job was won
  • Notes: Why lost, what changed, next steps

Recording outcomes

How to log what happened.

Not relevant

Mark as irrelevant if:

  • It's a small amendment (durability fix, minor change)
  • Building type doesn't fit (e.g., bare land subdivision)
  • NZ Foam product is not applicable

Action: Uncheck "Relevant" to exclude it from your pipeline count.

Relevant but not yet contacted

Leave as uncontacted if:

  • You are waiting for more information
  • The project is not yet at the right stage
  • You are prioritising other leads

Action: No outcome yet — leave contact date blank until you call.

Contacted — plans pending

You have reached the builder, PM or architect and they are sending plans or information.

Action: Record contact date. Set plans received to "No" until they arrive.

Plans received — quota'd

You have prepared a quote based on plans or site visit.

Action: Set plans received = "Yes". Enter quote value. Set outcome to "Quoted".

Won

Congratulations! The job was awarded to NZ Foam.

Action: Set outcome = "Won". Record actual won revenue.

Lost

The builder chose another supplier or abandoned the work.

Action: Set outcome = "Lost". Record why in notes (price, timing, specification, builder preference, project cancelled, etc.).

Important reminders

  • Data is local. Your browser stores reviews. Clear your cookies and reviews are lost. Export to CSV regularly.
  • No shared database yet. Reviews are not synced with the team. Discuss outcomes with Jacob to update team knowledge and score improvements.
  • Consent != confirmed insulation. Just because a consent was issued does not mean spray foam was specified. Always verify with the PM or architect.
  • Score is a guide. The fit score is indicative. Manual review of each project is still essential.
  • Contact early. The best time to influence insulation decisions is before the building envelope is designed. Application or approval stage is better than construction stage.
