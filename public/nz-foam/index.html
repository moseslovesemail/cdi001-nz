How to read an opportunity

Understanding the score and next steps.

Source facts

Read the description, council, reference, date, stage and published value. All records are from verified council sources.

Indicative fit

Description-based rules suggest review priority, not product specification or guaranteed suitability. Plan review is required.

Project contact

Open the project to see sourced organisations and contact routes where available. Applicant is not necessarily the decision-maker.

Your next action

Verify scope, timing and package responsibility before requesting plans or quoting. Record contact outcome and next step.

Typical workflow
01Find

Use filters to locate opportunities by fit score or review status in your area.

02Review

Read project details, consent stage, value and source confidence. Open project for contact routes.

03Contact

Call or email the listed contact. Confirm who owns the package and whether decisions remain open.

04Plans

Request architectural or engineering plans. Use them to assess fit and determine quote requirements.

05Quote

Submit quote from plans. Record value and mark contacted, plans received, and outcome.

06Track

Record outcome (Won, Lost, No fit), revenue won only after confirmed award, and notes for next action. Export reviews CSV.
