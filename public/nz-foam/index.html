NZ Foam fit score

Not every consent is a lead. The scoring layer decides what deserves sales attention.

30%Building / project type

Warehouse, agricultural, commercial roof, coolstore, specialty, residential and civil fit.

20%Construction signals

Steel frame, roof form, exposed cavities, thermal storage, modular/container or civil-fill indicators.

20%Project scale

Estimated value, floor area, units and likely install volume.

15%Consent stage

Earlier-stage projects may be more valuable when specifications can still change.

10%Geography / deployment

Nationwide operation allows scoring against team location and route efficiency.

5%Data confidence

Official source quality and completeness.

Sales workflow
01Consent appears

CDI detects a new or changed development signal.

02Classify + score

Project type, stage, scale and likely NZ Foam product fit.

03Assign lead

Route to the right regional salesperson or technical specialist.

04Get plans

Approach the builder, architect or project manager before insulation decisions are fixed.

05Quote + track

Quote from plans and record outcome to improve future scoring.

Design-partner objective

Use NZ Foam to prove CDI can generate attributable revenue.

The first useful measure is not database size. It is whether the radar identifies projects the sales team would otherwise have found later — or not at all.

For each opportunity, track: detected date, salesperson, contact date, plans received, quote value, outcome, lost reason and revenue won. That produces a direct CDI → pipeline → revenue case study.
