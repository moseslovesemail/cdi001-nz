How to read an opportunity

What the NZ Foam fit score means.

Project type

Warehouse, agricultural, commercial, specialty and residential fit. Score reflects building category and insulation likelihood.

Stage

Consent stage (application, approved, building consent issued). Earlier stages allow more specification influence.

Scale

Estimated project value, floor area and likely install volume indicate commercial relevance.

Confidence

Source quality and record completeness. Official council data only.

What the score does NOT claim:
  • Spray foam was specified or will be specified
  • The project will proceed or timelines are certain
  • Exact construction cost or budget accuracy
  • Applicant contact details are current or verified
  • Scoring is fully automated — manual review still needed
Typical workflow
01Find

Use filters to locate high-fit opportunities in your area or sector.

02Review

Read project details, consent stage, estimated value, and source confidence.

03Contact

Call or visit the builder, project manager or architect. Mark as contacted in your review.

04Plans

Request plans to assess insulation needs and determine quote requirements.

05Record

Log quote value, outcome and revenue. Export to share results with your team.

Important caveats

Data is incomplete

Coverage varies by council and consent type. Some major projects may not appear. Do not assume absence from the radar means no opportunity.

Browser storage only

Reviews are stored locally. Clear your browser data and reviews are lost. Export regularly to your sales tool or spreadsheet.

Score is indicative

The fit score is a guide, not a guarantee. Manual review of each project remains essential.

Consents change

Project scope, timeline and contractor decisions change frequently. Always verify current project status with the builder or PM.
