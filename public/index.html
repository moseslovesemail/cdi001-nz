The product

Council records describe regulatory events. CDI organises them around commercial opportunities.

A roofing company, HVAC contractor or supplier should not have to monitor multiple council websites, monthly registers and search systems to discover where development activity is emerging.

CDI collects, standardises and classifies those signals by location, consent stage, development type, scale and likely buyer relevance.

Important distinctions
Does a building consent mean construction has started?

No. CDI treats an issued building consent as a development/construction-intent signal. It does not by itself prove physical work has commenced.

Are resource-consent applications and granted consents treated the same?

No. Lodged, under consideration, granted, building-consent issued, construction and CCC/completed are separate stages. CDI separates observed facts from inferred status.

What am I paying for?

Collection, normalisation, classification, source tracking and a commercially useful view across fragmented council information — not resale of a council document as-is.
