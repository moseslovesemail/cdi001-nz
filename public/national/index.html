Where Project Radar gets project data.

Project Radar only includes verified public council sources. Records are reproduced from official council websites and registers with source links retained. No synthetic or inferred projects are included.

Data quality

What you can rely on.

Source verified

All records come from official council APIs, published registers, or verified reports. Source links are included so you can check the original.

Deduplicated

The same project is not shown twice even if it appears in multiple council systems.

Classified

Project type, scale, consent stage and NZ Foam fit are derived from council descriptions. Manual review remains essential.

Current

Data reflects the current live council source. Some councils update daily; others weekly or monthly.

Why use council sources

Council records describe regulatory intent. A building consent or resource consent means:

  • ✓ A specific project has been lodged with a council
  • ✓ The design, location and applicant are verified by an official
  • ✓ Estimated project value is often documented
  • ✗ Does NOT confirm spray foam is specified
  • ✗ Does NOT confirm the project will proceed on the stated timeline
  • ✗ Does NOT confirm current contact details are accurate

That's why Project Radar is a starting point: find verified development signals early, then contact the builder or PM to confirm relevance and get plans.
